Resiliency Through Redundancy One of the biggest advantages of the GO-2 systems over other electric propulsion systems is its resiliency. GO-2 uses 40 thrusters, each with its own dedicated power supply. In the rare case of an anomaly, the remaining thrusters are unaffected and will continue to operate at full capacity.
