Problem: LEO missions must meet 5-years post-mission disposal rules; using just drag can often be too slow. Solution: Use GO-2's large total impulse (up to –12.4 kNs) for an efficient and safe deorbit well within compliance. GO-2 Insights: Ideal for decommissioning satellites ranging from 6U to hundreds of kilograms small sats; satellites can decommission over many months to their desired altitude given the GO-2's long lifetime.
