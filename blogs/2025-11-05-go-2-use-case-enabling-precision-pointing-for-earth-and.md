Problem: High resolution imagers need high pointing accuracy with tight jitter control and rapid retargeting; reaction wheels alone can saturate or inject jitter. Solution: GO-2 provides continuous, adjustable low thrust to offload reaction wheels, damp jitter, and provides precise pointing with high pointing accuracy. GO-2 Insights: Fine thrust control and throttling across 5-400 μN with a minimum impulse bit of around 800 nNs enables gentle control during imaging windows.
